Student Life

Co-Curricular Clubs & Programs

Every student participates in at least two enrichment programs each semester to foster diverse interests.

Sports & Athletics

Competitive football, basketball, swimming, badminton, and track under certified national coaches.

Music & Band

Classical choir, strings orchestra, guitar, piano, drums, and student-led jazz fusion bands.

Dance & Movement

Contemporary choreography, classical ballet, hip-hop, and multicultural folk dance ensembles.

Visual Arts & Pottery

Acrylic and oil painting, clay sculpting, digital graphic design, and gallery exhibitions.

Drama & Theatre

Stage acting, scriptwriting, voice modulation, backstage lighting, and annual grand musical productions.

Robotics & AI

Microcontroller programming, sensor integration, LEGO Mindstorms, and national robotics league competitions.

Coding & App Dev

Python, HTML/CSS, game development in Unity, algorithmic logic, and annual hackathons.

Public Speaking & Debate

Parliamentary debate, persuasive oratory, impromptu speaking, and inter-school speech tournaments.

Eco & Science Club

Hydroponic campus gardening, renewable energy prototypes, astronomy nights, and eco-initiatives.

Community & Pride

The Four Houses of Bright Future

Our pastoral house system builds camaraderie, sportsmanship, and student leadership across all grade levels.

Phoenix House

Motto: "Rising Through Courage"

Fostering relentless perseverance, passion, and energetic team spirit.

Falcon House

Motto: "Aim Higher, Soar Further"

Emphasizing precision, visionary ambition, and academic mastery.

Titan House

Motto: "Strength Through Unity"

Championing integrity, defensive resilience, and collaborative teamwork.

Dragon House

Motto: "Wisdom & Honor"

Inspiring creativity, moral conviction, and insightful strategic leadership.
