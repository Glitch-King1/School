5-Step Admission Process

Our admission pathway is clear, transparent, and designed to ensure the best fit for your child's aspirations.

01

Online Enquiry

Complete the simple online enquiry form with your contact details and child's academic background.

02

Campus Tour

Visit our world-class campus, meet the counseling staff, and explore the smart learning spaces in action.

03

Interaction

A friendly, informal child-centric discussion or age-appropriate readiness evaluation by our faculty.

04

Documentation

Submit required identification, previous school report cards, birth certificate, and medical records.

05

Confirmation

Receive official admission confirmation letter, enrollment kit, and uniform/bus scheduling assistance.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to key questions parents frequently ask regarding admissions, fees, and campus life.

When do admissions open for the 2026–2027 academic year?

Admissions for the upcoming academic year are currently open. Because we maintain low student-teacher ratios (1:12), we encourage parents to apply early before grade cohorts reach capacity.

What is the teacher-to-student ratio in each classroom?

We maintain an average ratio of 1:12 in Early Years (with a dedicated lead teacher and learning assistant) and a maximum of 1:18 in Middle and Senior School cohorts, ensuring individualized support.

Does the school provide transportation and GPS bus tracking?

Yes, we operate a fleet of air-conditioned buses covering all major neighborhoods. All buses are equipped with real-time GPS tracking accessible via our parent mobile portal and trained female chaperones.

Are merit-based or talent scholarships available?

Bright Future offers competitive scholarships for students demonstrating exceptional academic brilliance, national sports achievement, or fine arts mastery entering Grades 8 through 11.

How does the school support students from non-English backgrounds?

We provide a dedicated English as an Additional Language (EAL) immersion track with specialized language coaches who assist transitioning students in small group workshops.
